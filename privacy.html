1. Introduction

Welcome to Cravier ("we," "our," or "us"). Cravier is a social restaurant discovery and posting mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information about you when you use the Cravier app ("App").

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

Cravier is operated by Tobey Lee, an individual developer based in Illinois, USA.

Cravier is currently in limited beta and is available only to users located in Illinois and Indiana. See Section 12.1 for how we determine service area eligibility.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the Cravier App. It does not apply to third-party websites or services that may be accessible through links within the App.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal information when you use Cravier:

2.1 Account and Identity Information

  • Email address (collected via Firebase Authentication when you register)
  • Display name or username (chosen by you at account setup)
  • Profile photo (uploaded by you, optional)
  • If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email address, we receive only the private relay address Apple generates for you, not your actual email address

2.2 User-Generated Content

  • Posts and captions you create and publish within the App
  • Restaurant reviews and ratings you submit
  • Photos and videos you upload or capture using your device camera
  • Comments you write on your own or other users' posts
  • Reports you submit about content, accounts, or restaurant information

2.3 Restaurant Interaction Data

  • Restaurants you save, view, like, or interact with within the App
  • Taps, searches, and browsing activity related to restaurant discovery features
  • Cuisine and preference signals inferred from these interactions, used to personalize your recommendations

2.4 Location Data

  • Precise GPS location data, collected only when you grant the App location permission
  • Precise location is used to display and search for restaurants near you, and is not stored in that form
  • Your state or region, used to confirm the App is available in your area during our limited beta (see Section 12.1)
  • A single approximate location per account, rounded on your device to roughly 11 kilometres, used for internal product planning (see Section 12.2)
  • You may deny or revoke location permission at any time through your iOS device settings

2.5 Device and Camera Access

  • Camera access: requested only when you choose to take a photo or video for a post; no media is accessed without your initiation
  • Photo library access: requested only when you choose to upload media from your device library

2.6 Analytics and Diagnostic Data

  • App usage events (e.g., features used, screens visited, buttons tapped)
  • Session data (e.g., session length, frequency of use)
  • Crash reports and diagnostics collected via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Device type, operating system version, and other technical identifiers associated with analytics data

2.7 Content Safety Screening

Photos and video cover images you upload are automatically screened for prohibited content before they are published. This screening is performed by the Google Cloud Vision API. See Section 5.3 for details on how this data is handled.

2.8 Payment Information

Cravier does not currently offer paid subscriptions or in-app purchases, and we do not collect or process payment information of any kind. If we introduce paid features in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy before doing so and describe the payment processing arrangements at that time.

2.9 Information Not Collected

We do not collect the following:

  • Social Security numbers or government-issued identification numbers
  • Precise biometric data
  • Health or medical information
  • Payment card numbers or financial account details
  • Location history or background location
  • Data about children under the age of 13 (see Section 10)

4.5 With Your Consent

5. Third-Party Services

Cravier integrates with the following third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy.

5.1 Google Firebase

Firebase provides authentication, database (Firestore), file storage, server-side functions, analytics, and crash reporting services. Data processed through Firebase is subject to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5.2 Google Maps Platform / Google Places API

We use Google Maps and Google Places API to enable restaurant search and display within the App. Your search queries and location data may be transmitted to Google's servers. Google Privacy Policy

5.3 Google Cloud Vision API

When you publish a post, the images you have uploaded are sent to the Google Cloud Vision API for automated safety screening before the post becomes visible to others. For videos, only the cover frame is screened.

This screening returns a likelihood rating for categories of prohibited content. It does not perform facial recognition, does not identify individuals, and is not used to build a profile of you. Images are sent for the purpose of this check only. Google Privacy Policy

5.4 Expo Push Notifications / Firebase Cloud Messaging

Push notifications are delivered via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), integrated through Expo's push notification infrastructure. To deliver notifications, your device's push notification token is transmitted to FCM. You can disable push notifications at any time through your iOS device settings (Settings > Notifications > Cravier).

5.5 Sign in with Apple

If you choose to sign in with Apple, Apple provides us with an identifier for your account and, depending on your choice, either your email address or a private relay address that forwards to you. Apple's handling of this information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

9. Your Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA and CalOPPA)

This section describes privacy rights available under California law, including the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA) and the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA). These rights are available to California residents. However, we extend these rights as a matter of policy to all users of the App regardless of residency.

9.1 California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) Rights

California residents have the following rights with respect to their personal information:

Right to Know. You have the right to request that we disclose: (a) the categories of personal information we have collected about you; (b) the categories of sources from which we collected your personal information; (c) our business or commercial purpose for collecting your personal information; (d) the categories of third parties with whom we share your personal information; and (e) the specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you.

Right to Delete. You have the right to request that we delete personal information we have collected about you, subject to certain exceptions. We will comply with verified deletion requests unless retention is necessary to: complete a transaction, detect security incidents, debug errors, exercise free speech or other legal rights, comply with a legal obligation, or for other purposes permitted by the CCPA/CPRA.

Right to Correct. You have the right to request that we correct inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.

Right to Opt-Out of Sale or Sharing. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising purposes. Accordingly, there is no opt-out mechanism required for this practice at this time. If our practices change, we will update this policy and provide the required opt-out mechanism.

Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information. The CCPA/CPRA treats precise geolocation as sensitive personal information. We use precise location only to fulfill searches you initiate and to confirm service-area eligibility, and we do not retain it. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes beyond those permitted by the CCPA/CPRA.

Right to Non-Discrimination. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA/CPRA rights. We will not deny you goods or services, charge different prices, provide a different level of service, or suggest that you will receive a different level of service because you exercised your privacy rights.

12. Location Data

The App may request access to your device's precise location (GPS) to enable the nearby restaurant search feature. Location access is entirely optional and controlled by you through your iOS device settings.

We collect location data only when: (a) you have granted the App permission to access your location; and (b) you are actively using the App. We do not track your location in the background.

Precise location is transmitted to our servers and to the Google Maps / Google Places API solely to fulfill the restaurant search request you initiate, and is not retained after the request is served. We do not use your location data to build profiles for advertising purposes.

You can revoke location permission at any time by navigating to: Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Cravier on your iOS device.

12.1 Service Area Verification

Cravier is currently in limited beta and available only in Illinois and Indiana. When you open the App, we determine which state you are in so we can confirm the App is available to you. We store only the outcome of this check and the approximate location described in Section 12.2 — not the precise reading used to make the determination.

If location permission is not granted, we cannot verify your service area, and access to the App may be limited during the beta period.

12.2 Approximate Usage Location

To understand which areas our service is used in, we store a single approximate location for your account. This value is rounded on your device to roughly 11 kilometres before it is transmitted, meaning it identifies a general metropolitan area rather than an address, building, or route.

We keep exactly one such record per account. It is overwritten in place rather than accumulated, and is updated no more than once per day. As a result, we do not hold location history, and the stored data cannot be used to reconstruct your movements or determine when you use the App. When this data is reviewed internally, it is aggregated so that individual accounts are not identifiable.

This information is used solely for internal product planning — for example, deciding which regions to expand into next. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

You can stop this collection at any time by revoking location permission, and you can remove the stored record by deleting your account.

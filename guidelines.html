CRAVIER

Community Guidelines

Effective Date: March 25, 2026  ·  Last Reviewed: March 25, 2026

Contact: cravierapp@gmail.com

Cravier is a place to discover restaurants and share real food experiences with people around you. These guidelines keep it a place people can trust. By using Cravier, you agree to follow them. Violating these guidelines may result in content removal, account restrictions, or a permanent ban, at our discretion.

1. Be Real

  • Post your own photos, videos, and reviews of places you've actually been. Don't post content that isn't yours, misrepresent where or when a photo was taken, or pass off someone else's experience as your own.
  • Don't create fake accounts, impersonate another person or business, or operate multiple accounts to manipulate ratings, likes, or follower counts.
  • Restaurant owners and staff: you're welcome here, but don't post reviews of your own business or a competitor's, and don't offer incentives (discounts, free food, cash) in exchange for a review. Reviews need to reflect a real, independent experience.

2. Be Respectful

  • No harassment, bullying, threats, or targeted abuse of any user, restaurant, or staff member.
  • No hate speech or content that attacks people based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, or veteran status.
  • Disagreements about food and service happen — critique the experience, not the person. Personal attacks on restaurant staff by name are not allowed.
  • No sharing another person's private information (home address, phone number, workplace, etc.) without their consent.

3. Keep It Food-Focused

  • Cravier is for restaurants, food, and dining experiences. Off-topic spam, unrelated promotions, referral/pyramid schemes, and mass-tagging are not allowed.
  • No repetitive or bulk posting designed to game the algorithm or flood the feed.
  • Sponsored or paid content must be disclosed and will be labeled as "Sponsored" — if you're a business paying for placement, we handle that labeling; don't attempt to disguise paid content as an organic post.

4. Keep It Safe

  • No nudity, sexually explicit content, or content that sexualizes minors. This applies with zero tolerance and zero exceptions.
  • No graphic violence, gore, or content that threatens or glorifies harm to people or animals.
  • No promotion of illegal activity, including the sale of illegal substances or goods.
  • Content involving alcohol is allowed in a food/dining context (e.g., a restaurant's drink menu) but may not encourage excessive or underage drinking.
  • No content that promotes self-harm, eating disorders, or dangerous challenges.

5. Respect Ownership

  • Only post content you own or have permission to share. Don't repost another creator's photos or videos as your own.
  • Give credit where it's due — if you're resharing or referencing someone else's content, tag them.
  • Restaurant logos, menus, and branding belong to the business; use them respectfully and don't misrepresent an affiliation you don't have.

6. Reviews & Ratings

  • Rate and review based on your actual experience — food, service, atmosphere, value.
  • Don't manipulate ratings by review-bombing a business (positively or negatively) in coordination with others.
  • If you have a dispute with a business about a review, use the in-app reporting tools rather than escalating outside the app.

7. Minors on Cravier

  • You must be at least 13 years old to use Cravier.
  • Content that endangers, exploits, or targets minors will be removed immediately and may be reported to the appropriate authorities.

8. Reporting & Enforcement

If you see content or behavior that violates these guidelines:

  • Tap the "•••" menu on any post, comment, or profile and select Report, or
  • Go to Settings → Support → Report a Problem, or
  • Email us directly at cravierapp@gmail.com

We review reports and take action that may include:

  • Removing the content
  • Issuing a warning
  • Temporarily restricting an account (posting, commenting, or messaging)
  • Permanently banning an account for severe or repeated violations

You can manage your own experience at any time using Settings → Privacy & Safety → Blocked Users to block anyone you don't want to interact with.

9. Changes to These Guidelines

We may update these guidelines as Cravier grows. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated guidelines.
